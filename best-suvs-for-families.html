Best Family SUVs: Safety Ratings, Cargo Space & Comfort

By Sarah Linwood • Published October 12, 2026 • 5 min read • IIHS Top Safety Picks
Modern three-row SUVs blend acoustic luxury, advanced driver-assist systems (ADAS), and flexible cargo configurations for long-distance family travel.

Choosing the right family sport utility vehicle is about far more than horsepower figures or curb appeal. When transporting children, cargo, and pets on cross-country holidays or daily school commutes, structural crash-test integrity, intuitive child-seat latch geometry, and rear-cabin climate zoning take top priority.

IIHS Top Safety Pick+ Criteria: What Matters Most

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) recently updated its moderate overlap front crash and side impact evaluations to incorporate heavier testing barriers that simulate impacts with modern high-riding trucks and heavy EV crossovers. Look for models earning 'Good' ratings across all sub-categories, alongside standard pedestrian automatic emergency braking that functions in darkness.

Child Safety Seat LATCH Score

Ensure the second-row tether anchors and lower LATCH rings are easily reachable without tearing into stiff leather upholstery seams, and verify that the middle seat allows three-across child seat configurations if you have a growing family.

Third-Row Legroom and Usability

Many three-row crossovers look spacious on brochures but relegate rear occupants to knees-to-chest torture. True full-size crossovers like the Toyota Grand Highlander and Kia Telluride provide over 33 inches of legitimate adult third-row legroom and allow easy walk-through access via tilt-and-slide captain's chairs even with a child seat installed in the second row.

Hybrid vs Traditional V6 Powertrains

For stop-and-go suburban driving, hybrid powertrains deliver dramatic savings. A hybrid SUV returning 35 MPG cuts annual fuel expenditures in half compared to a naturally aspirated 3.8-liter V6 delivering 18 MPG in heavy city traffic, saving the average household over $1,200 per year.
